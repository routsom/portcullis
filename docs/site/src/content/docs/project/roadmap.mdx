## Beyond 1.0 — the open platform

Everything below is **Apache-2.0**. Per Directive #3 and our governance rule,
*anything a company needs is built in the open, or not at all.* These are
capabilities, not a paid tier.

### Observability & response

- **Web console** — a read-only-by-default UI over live traffic, the audit
  trail, per-tenant metrics, and the capability catalog. It *proposes* config
  diffs; it never holds authoritative state (config stays in files).
- **Anomaly detection** — behavioural baselines per `(tenant, principal,
  capability)` that flag the unusual: volume spikes, brand-new destinations,
  argument-shape drift, off-hours access, sudden spend.
- **Alerting** — first-class routes to Slack, PagerDuty, Opsgenie, and webhooks
  on policy denials over a threshold, circuit trips, rug-pull / poisoning hits,
  budget breaches, **audit-chain verification failures**, and sandbox escape
  attempts.
- **Fleet kill switch** — one command (or one tap from your phone) pauses tool
  execution across every gateway in the org, instantly. Contain the blast radius
  first, investigate second.
- **Session forensics & replay** — reconstruct exactly what a principal did and
  what a compromised tool *could* have reached, with the signed audit chain as
  the source of truth.

### Governance & scale

- **Policy-as-code / GitOps** — versioned policy bundles with staging, canary
  rollout, diff review, and instant rollback; `policy test` against recorded
  traffic before you ship a rule.
- **Approval workflows** — human-in-the-loop gates for high-risk capabilities and
  rug-pull re-approvals, fully audited, approvable from Slack or email.
- **Cost & usage analytics** — per-team token/request accounting, budgets, and
  chargeback reports.
- **SIEM & OpenTelemetry export** — stream the signed audit chain and metrics to
  Splunk, Datadog, Elastic, S3, and OTel collectors, with tamper-evident chain
  proofs.
- **Open threat-intel format** — a signature format for poisoned tools and
  known-bad MCP servers, so anyone can publish and consume feeds.
- **Delegation & agent-to-agent** — richer delegation-chain policy, per-hop
  budgets, and provenance across multi-agent call graphs.

## portcullis Cloud — the business model

:::note[How this stays free]
The gateway is, and always will be, **Apache-2.0 with no feature paywall**. We
monetize the way durable open source does — **hosting, operations, curated data,
and people** — never by locking capabilities. Everything a company *needs* is in
the open; the Cloud is convenience, scale, curated intelligence, and expertise.
:::

you safe, it's in the binary, for free. The Cloud sells the operational burden
you'd rather not carry.
